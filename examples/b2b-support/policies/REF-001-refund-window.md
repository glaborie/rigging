# Policy REF-001 — Refund eligibility window

A business customer may request a refund within **30 calendar days** of the
delivery date recorded on the order. After 30 days, refunds require a written
commercial exception approved by an account manager — the order desk cannot
grant one and must escalate.

Partial refunds are permitted for partially delivered orders: refund only the
delivered line items, at the unit price actually charged.
