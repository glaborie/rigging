# Policy PRI-001 — Volume tier pricing

Unit prices fall with quantity tiers: 1–9 units at list price, 10–49 at 95%
of list, 50–199 at 90%, 200+ at 85%. Tiers apply per line item per order;
they cannot be combined across orders.

Custom quotes below the 85% floor require a commercial decision — do not
commit; escalate.
